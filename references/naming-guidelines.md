# Video Game Naming Guidelines & Ideation Strategies

This document provides principles for brainstorming, selecting, and refining distinctive video game titles that are memorable, searchable, and less prone to legal conflicts.

---

## 1. Attributes of Strong Game Titles

- **Distinctive & Ownable:** Avoid generic combinations of fantasy or sci-fi clichés.
- **Search Engine Friendly (SEO):** A unique coined word or unexpected pairing dominates search engine results immediately, whereas generic words ("Dark War") compete against millions of unrelated pages.
- **Pronounceable & Spoken-Friendly:** Streamers, podcasters, and players must be able to pronounce and spell it easily on voice chat.
- **Tone & Genre Alignment:** The title should evoke the atmosphere and emotional core of the game.
- **Franchise Scalability:** Can the title cleanly accommodate subtitles, sequels, or spin-offs?

---

## 2. Naming Styles & Patterns

### Style 1: Short & Punchy (Single Evocative Word)
- *Examples:* *Hades*, *Portal*, *Celeste*, *Braid*, *Inscryption*
- *Strengths:* Instant brand recall, clean logo geometry, memorable.
- *Risks:* High trademark crowding; common dictionary words are hard to protect.

### Style 2: Two-Word Evocative Compounds
- *Examples:* *Hollow Knight*, *Dead Cells*, *Frostpunk*, *Subnautica*
- *Strengths:* Striking imagery, easier to trademark, strong SEO footprint.
- *Tip:* Combine one concrete physical word with one emotional or contrasting concept.

### Style 3: Invented / Coined Portmanteaus
- *Examples:* *Minecraft*, *Spore*, *Factorio*, *Kerbal*
- *Strengths:* Highest legal defensibility, zero competition on Google, perfect for domain availability.

### Style 4: Narrative / Phrase-Based
- *Examples:* *Don't Starve*, *A Plague Tale*, *We Happy Few*, *Outer Wilds*
- *Strengths:* Intrigues the player with immediate premise and atmosphere.

---

## 3. Red Flags & Common Pitfalls

1. **The "Trope Salad" Trap:**
   - Avoid generic pairs: *War of Shadows*, *Legends of the Dark*, *Dragon's Blood*.
   - These are forgettable, unprotectable, and invite endless confusion.

2. **Accidental Brand Proximity:**
   - Don't borrow distinct elements from titan franchises (e.g., using "Scrolls", "Craft", "Fallout", "Souls", "Fortnite"). Even if legally defensible, it invites cease-and-desist letters or trademark opposition.

3. **Unintended Foreign Translations:**
   - Check proposed invented words across major languages (Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Turkish) to avoid unintended slang or vulgarities.

4. **SEO Homonym Nightmares:**
   - Naming a game after a common software tool, common animal, or everyday object (*Word*, *Apple*, *Table*, *Go*) ensures your game is unfindable on search engines.

---

## 4. The 5-Step Brainstorm & Verification Loop

1. **Extract Core DNA:** List 5-10 core attributes (mechanics, setting, mood, emotional arc).
2. **Generate Broad Pool (20+ Candidates):** Brainstorm across all 4 styles above without judging.
3. **Internal Filter (Down to 5):** Eliminate clunky, hard-to-spell, or derivative names.
4. **Automated Skill Check:** Run `python -m scripts check "Candidate"` on each shortlist item.
5. **Select & Trademark Verify:** Choose the lowest-risk candidate and perform manual registry checks.
